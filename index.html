About Zana Auto & Heavy Equipments Trading Inc.

View Ceremonial Turnover

Zana Auto & Heavy Equipments Trading Inc. is built on a foundation of quality, official procurement integrity, and excellence. We are a premier provider of government municipal machinery, heavy trucks, auto repair, tire sales, and commercial automotive solutions in the Visayas.

From supplying Sinotruk HOWO trucks, XCMG excavators, and emergency water tankers to LGUs, to delivering master computerized diagnostics and fleet care at our Cebu hub, we ensure every vehicle meets top performance and safety standards.

Visit our main workshop at 175-N. Bacalso. ave, Cebu City or consult with our procurement specialists for official equipment bidding and fleet orders.
